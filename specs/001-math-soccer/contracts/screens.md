# Contract: screens, keys and test hooks

World size 1600 by 1000, scaled to fit (src/core/match/world.ts, amendment 02). Goal frame (outer edge of posts and crossbar): x 260 to 1340, y 200 to 800, posts and crossbar 22 thick; the bottom edge is the goal line. Number chart panel inside the goal mouth: x 340 to 1260, y 265 to 750, so the margin at the sides (80) and the top (65) is at least 6% of the goal width (FR-054). Stands: y 0 to 260. Ball spot before a shot (kick spot): (860, 940), below the goal line.

Scene additions after amendments 08 to 14:

- Player (amendment 08, FR-082 to FR-084): at least 1.3 times the keeper's height, seen from behind, to the lower left of the ball; the bottom edge of the screen may cut off his lower legs, and he never covers a chart square. The kick moves him one step toward the ball and back (about 0.3 s).
- Row and column scales (amendment 10, FR-091 to FR-095; amendment 14, FR-115): the row scale stands right of the right post, one label per chart row (0, 10, 20 …); the column scale stands above the crossbar, labels 1 to 10. Each lies on one calm dark strip (FR-101a). They are hidden while the child aims without help: the row scale shows from help step 1, the column scale from step 2, and both go when the target is scored or replaced. With "Zeilen- und Spaltenhilfe" off a shown scale has only its lit label.
- Net and chart (amendment 11, FR-096 to FR-098): a diamond net in thin chalk lines that bulges around the ball for about 0.4 s on a goal; the chart cells form a soft panel that reads apart from the net. The top bar has a solid night-sky background, so no floodlight shows behind it (FR-099).

Scene additions after amendments 18 to 21:

- Cutouts (amendment 18, FR-124 to FR-130): at level 6 a cutout's empty squares are filled one after the other. A correct number on a square that is not the last one lands on it, lights it up with its number and the fans clap; the keeper waits at the side and dives only for the last square, which is a normal goal.
- Cup lift (amendment 19, FR-131): both arms raised, the hands holding the cup from below above the head.
- Locker room looks (amendment 20, FR-134 to FR-139): the ball trail follows each shot and fades within 0.4 s after landing; the goal effect plays about 1 s on every goal (the cup final keeps its own show); the mascot stands left of the goal, jumps on a goal and covers its eyes on a miss; the scoreboard shows the stadium name next to the goal counter; bigger stands, the floodlight color and the weather (evening, day, sunset, snow, rain) change the backdrop, and snow and rain fall behind the chart.
- "Freistoß mit Mauer" (amendment 21, FR-142): 3 to 5 defenders, taller than the keeper, stand on the grass in front of the goal line under the columns they cover, drawn behind the grid. A shot behind the wall hits the wall.
- "Selbst ins Tor" (amendment 21, FR-143 to FR-146): the child is the keeper in his kit and shirt number; an opponent in a cup team's colors stands at the kick spot. The target square flashes with no number and a countdown ring in kit yellow; the ball rolls toward it for the flight time and stops while help holds it.
- "Elfmeterschießen" (amendment 21, FR-147, FR-148): on the child's turn he shoots as in "Spielen" against a keeper in the opponent team's colors; on the opponent's turn the shot plays as in "Selbst ins Tor". A won shootout brings confetti, fireworks and the cup lift.

## Test query parameters

These parameters exist for the end-to-end tests, as in math-tank. A child never needs them.

- `?start=play|time|cup|wall|keeper|shootout`: what Enter or Space on the start screen starts: "Spielen" (`play`, also without the parameter), the time challenge (`time`, only when the parent allows it), the cup's next match without the bracket screen (`cup`), or an unlocked mode the parent allows (amendment 21). It replaces `?mode=`, which went with the hole and line modes (amendment 05).
- `?seed=N`: the round seed, a whole number from 0 to 9999999999, so targets follow the same order on every run.
- `?speed=N`: the test speed-up hook (T092). Play time runs N times as fast (0 to 1000; 0 stops it). With the parameter present, a spec can change the speed while the page runs by setting `window.mathSoccerSpeed`, for example to score at normal speed and then let a time challenge run out. Without the parameter, `window.mathSoccerSpeed` is ignored.
- Short rounds: specs store settings before the page loads (`seedStorage` in `tests/e2e/fixtures.ts`), for example `roundType: 'goalCount'` with `goalsPerRound: 2`, instead of a query parameter.
- A running round is saved under `mathsoccer.round.v1` between shots, so a reload continues it.
- `<html data-version>`: the running build (APP_VERSION in tests, the short git commit otherwise), set by `src/main.tsx` for the update test (`tests/e2e/update.spec.ts`, amendment 12).
