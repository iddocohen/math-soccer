# Critique Report: Math Soccer

**Date**: 2026-11-06 | **Inputs**: [spec.md](../spec.md), [plan.md](../plan.md), [source-prd.md](../source-prd.md) | **Constitution**: unfilled template, no principles apply

## Executive Summary

The spec and plan solve a real, observed problem: one child who handles ±1 on the hundred chart but not ±10 and the diagonals. Scope is tight, and the plan reuses a proven sibling project. Two items must be fixed before tasks: hole mode has several holes but no rule for a shot into a non-target hole, and the goal and star outcome of a shot after help step 3 is undefined. The parent's feedback on the prototype (the keeper must dive toward the ball) is also folded in. Verdict: **PROCEED WITH UPDATES**.
